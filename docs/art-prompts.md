# Prompts de arte: temas por classe do EAD Skill Preview

Ferramenta: Gemini Nano Banana. Proporção **3:4** (retrato), maior resolução disponível.

Para cada classe: anexe `art-src/layout-reference.png` e cole o prompt da classe
inteiro. Cada prompt abaixo é completo (estrutura + visual da classe).

Na imagem de estrutura: cinza = janela e suas peças; magenta = fundo; manchas
ciano = onde entram os enfeites (canto superior direito, cantos inferiores e as
duas laterais).

Salve cada resultado em `art-src/<Classe>.png` com o nome indicado no título.
Gere primeiro o Guerreiro e mande para conferência antes de gerar as outras.

## Guerreiro (`Warrior.png`)

```
The attached image is a grey blockout of a game interface window. Use
it as the exact layout and repaint it as a finished fantasy MMORPG interface
window. Output in the same 3:4 portrait framing, with every shape in the same
position, size and proportion as in the blockout.

How to read the blockout:
- Magenta = background. Keep it pure flat magenta #FF00FF everywhere outside
  the window: no gradient, no shadow, no glow, no drop shadow.
- Large mid-grey rounded rectangle = the window frame. Paint it with the
  frame materials from LOOK.
- Light grey circle at the top-left = a round medallion with an embossed
  emblem (see LOOK).
- Wide dark-grey bar at the top = a blank, slightly recessed title plate.
  Leave it completely empty.
- Light grey circle with an X at the top-right = a round close button with a
  small X.
- Dark-grey bar below the title, on the left = an empty recessed strip. Leave
  it plain: no buttons, no dividers.
- Pill with a chevron on the right = a drop-down field: dark inside, thin
  metal rim, one small chevron pointing down at its right end.
- Long pill below = a search field: dark inside, thin metal rim, a small
  magnifying glass at its right end.
- Large near-black rectangle = the list panel: thin decorative rim, small
  corner pieces, and a plain, dark, calm interior (brightness below 25%) with
  only a very faint material texture. Nothing painted inside it.
- Dark-grey bar at the bottom = an empty footer strip of the frame material.
- Cyan blobs = position markers only. They are NOT objects, plates, backdrops
  or shadows. Delete each cyan blob completely and paint, at that spot, the
  small objects listed in LOOK. The oval shape itself must not appear in the
  result in any color: no oval, disc, dark patch, halo or glow behind or
  around the objects. Wherever the blob was and the object does not cover,
  show what belongs there: pure flat magenta outside the window, the normal
  window frame inside it. Each object is small and fits inside the area its
  blob marked. Light sources such as candles or crystals cast no glow onto
  the magenta. Put no decoration anywhere else.
- The list panel is off limits: no decoration may overlap the list panel or
  its rim, not even by a little. The two bottom objects sit entirely below
  the bottom edge of the list panel; the two side objects sit entirely
  outside its left and right edges.

Rules: no text, no letters, no numbers, no writing-like runes, no characters,
no creatures, no icons inside the panels. Perfectly flat front view, no
perspective. Decorations never touch the image border and never cover the
title plate, the strip, the drop-down field, the search field, the close
button or the list panel interior.

DARK THEME: the whole window is dark. Frame materials stay dark (brightness
below 35%); light and bright colors appear only as thin accents on rims,
gems and small details. No white, ivory, cream or pale frame.

STYLE: stylized hand-painted fantasy game UI in the spirit of World of
Warcraft and Hearthstone interface art. Chunky, slightly exaggerated shapes
with thick beveled edges and rounded corners; heavy metal trim, oversized
rivets and gems; visible painterly brush strokes; saturated, warm colors with
strong highlights and deep soft shadows that make every piece look carved
and solid. Painted shading only: no black ink outlines, no cel shading, no
flat vector look, no photorealism. Sharp and highly detailed.

LOOK - WARRIOR: a war room of a veteran soldier. Frame of dark forged steel
plates with rivets and battle scratches, bound with worn red-brown leather
straps. Accent color: warm tan bronze (#C79C6E) on rims, medallion and close
button. List panel interior: very dark charcoal leather. Medallion emblem: two
crossed swords. Decorations: top-right, a sword and a battle axe crossed;
bottom-left, a battered round shield; bottom-right, a short length of iron
chain; left edge, a torn dark red war banner; right edge, a steel pauldron
with leather straps.
```
